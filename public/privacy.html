Heygotchu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Heygotchu ("we", "the app") is a small outfit-planning app for personal and family use, available at heygotchu.com and as a mobile app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password).
  • Closet data: the clothing items you add, including photos you upload, their attributes, your clothing preferences, and saved trip/occasion plans.
  • Optional color palette: if you choose to add a selfie, it is analyzed to derive a clothing color palette. The selfie itself is never stored — it is processed in memory and discarded; only the derived palette (undertone, best colors, suggested wardrobe department) is saved to your account, and you can change or ignore it anytime.

How AI processing works

Photos you upload (clothing photos and the optional selfie) and your closet/preference data are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to power features like automatic photo tagging, outfit suggestions, color analysis, and trip planning. This data is used only to generate your results.

Where data lives

  • Account and closet data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Neon) and served through our API on secured cloud infrastructure.
  • Verification and password-reset emails are delivered via Resend.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling or sharing of your data with third parties beyond the service providers listed above.
  • Your closet, plans, and palette are visible only to you — and to the family administrator account that manages this private app.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data (closet items, photos, preferences, saved plans) at any time from inside the app: Preferences → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also request deletion by emailing the address below.
