Delete your Heygotchu account

Last updated: 31 August 2026

You can permanently delete your Heygotchu account and everything in it — closet photos, items, preferences, and saved trip/occasion plans — at any time. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Delete it yourself (in the app or on the web)

  1. Go to heygotchu.com and log in (or open the Heygotchu app).
  2. Tap Preferences in the navigation.
  3. Scroll to Delete my account and tap it.
  4. Confirm by tapping Yes, delete my account.
Log in to delete my account

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Every closet item and its photo
  • Your clothing preferences and derived color palette
  • Saved trips, occasions, and reminders

Can't log in?

Email musahidqurbani@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm your identity and remove your account and data within 30 days.

This page exists to meet Google Play's account-deletion requirement. For details on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
