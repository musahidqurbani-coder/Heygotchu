## Full description (4000 chars max)
Heygotchu turns the clothes you already own into perfectly planned outfits.

📸 SNAP YOUR CLOSET
Photograph your wardrobe — even a whole outfit in one shot. Heygotchu's AI detects each piece (top, bottom, scarf, hat, jewelry), crops it into its own picture, reads its color, sleeve length, warmth and style, and files everything neatly into your digital closet. Duplicates are caught automatically.

🎨 YOUR PERSONAL COLOR PALETTE
Add an optional selfie and Heygotchu works out the clothing colors that suit you best — your photo is analyzed and immediately discarded, only the palette is saved.

✈️ TRIP PLANNING
Tell it where you're going. Heygotchu checks the weather, builds a day-by-day outfit plan from your own clothes, writes a packing list, and even sketches a mini travel itinerary sized to your trip.

🎉 OCCASION STYLING
Sangeet, Mehndi, Haldi, Nikah, Eid, Diwali, weddings, interviews, brunches — pick from 50+ occasions and get complete outfit combinations from your closet, cross-matched across everything you own, with culture-aware styling tips and ideas for pieces worth adding.

🧕 MODESTY-AWARE
A dedicated hijabi mode and coverage preferences ensure every recommendation respects how you dress — full sleeves, necklines, hem lengths, and modest swimwear included.

👨‍👩‍👧 MADE FOR FAMILIES
Each family member gets their own private closet, palette, and plans.

You choose the vibe — we gotchu. �ᰔ
